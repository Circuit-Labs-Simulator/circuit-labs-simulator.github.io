# Circuit Lab

A circuit simulator that runs in the browser. Drag out batteries, light bulbs, wires, resistors and switches, connect them, and watch the electrons move.

**Open it:** https://rishik-sundar.github.io/circuit-lab/

- Change the battery voltage and the resistance of bulbs and resistors
- Wire Resistance mode: longer and thinner wires slow the current down
- Series vs Parallel example: series bulbs are dimmer, parallel drains the battery faster
- Ammeter, voltmeter and ohmmeter you can drag around
- Electron flow or conventional current
